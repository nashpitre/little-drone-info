Little Drone privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026. Little Drone is an independent Android gaming companion maintained by Nash Pitre. Contact: nashpitre@gmail.com.

Google account and spreadsheet access

Google connection is optional and separate from ChatGPT. Little Drone requests the drive.file permission, which grants access to files you explicitly select through Google’s picker. It reads your Google account email to identify the connected account, selected spreadsheet metadata, worksheet headers and game rows needed to match records and verify updates.

After you choose the worksheet and enable sync, the app can update existing playing status, last-played date and estimated story-completion fields. It can create a Little Drone worksheet for reviewed story beat, current objective, earlier-story summary, progress estimate and update time. Ambiguous game editions require a one-time row link. Sync estimates are not exact save-file completion. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Google spreadsheet content is not sent to the AI provider.

Screen, voice and AI

With Android screen-sharing permission and your in-app preview approval, the app holds screenshots in memory. When you request help or enable Live, resized game frames and relevant local game context are sent to the AI connection you selected: your eligible ChatGPT connection or an explicitly configured OpenAI API key. Those services process data under their own privacy terms and account controls. Live can consume account usage or API credit. No Google tokens are sent to OpenAI.

Microphone questions use offline voice recognition. Permitted game-playback audio can also be transcribed offline. Raw audio is processed on the device; recognized question text or game dialogue may become part of the game context sent to your chosen AI connection. Optional screen-text recognition is local. Spoken assistant responses are off by default.

Local storage and security

Conversation history, per-game memory, reviewed progress and story interpretation are stored privately on the device. ChatGPT credentials and optional API keys are encrypted using Android Keystore. Google manages its authorization cache; Little Drone holds Google access tokens in process memory and does not persist Google access or refresh tokens. Selected account email, spreadsheet connection, row links and pending sync records are stored privately on the device. Android backup is disabled. The app has no publisher-operated account or game-data backend and no advertising or analytics SDK.

Your controls and deletion

You can turn Live off, stop screen sharing, disable dialogue capture and change sync options. Disconnect Google in Settings to cancel the sync queue, clear the local Google connection and request revocation of the app’s Google grant. You can also remove access through your Google account’s third-party access controls. Disconnecting does not delete records already written to your own spreadsheet; you control those records in Google Sheets.

You can forget an individual game in the app, clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove local app data. Previously synced spreadsheet records remain until you delete them yourself. Sign out of ChatGPT separately to clear that connection. The publisher does not receive your chat history or spreadsheet and cannot delete data held in your Google or OpenAI accounts on your behalf.

Limited use

Little Drone’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the visible spreadsheet connection and sync features you enable.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. Contact support with privacy questions.
