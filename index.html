Little Drone

A quiet story companion for Android gaming handhelds, designed for the AYN Thor’s bottom screen.

Connect your eligible ChatGPT account for screenshot-based help. Optionally connect Google and choose your games spreadsheet to sync reviewed story progress as you play.

Google access is limited to files you explicitly select. You review the worksheet and sync options before enabling updates. Android screen sharing always requires your permission.

This is an independent app. It is not affiliated with Google, OpenAI, AYN or any game publisher.
